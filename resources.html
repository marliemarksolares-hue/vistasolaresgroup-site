What are the types of business taxes in the Philippines?
  • Income Tax
  • Value-Added Tax (VAT), 12%
  • Percentage Tax, 3% for non-VAT taxpayers
  • Withholding Tax
  • Excise Tax
Who qualifies as a micro taxpayer?

A micro taxpayer has gross sales not exceeding PHP 3 million. Micro taxpayers are exempt from VAT and subject to the 3% percentage tax, with the option to choose VAT. Under the EOPT classification: Micro is below ₱3M, Small is ₱3M to below ₱20M, Medium is ₱20M to below ₱1B, and Large is ₱1B and above.

What is the 8% flat income tax option for small businesses?

If your gross sales are below PHP 3 million, you may pay 8% on gross sales or receipts instead of the graduated income tax rates. It is beneficial when your business has minimal expenses.

How do I register my business with the Bureau of Internal Revenue (BIR)?
  1. Register your business name or entity: DTI for sole proprietors, SEC for corporations and partnerships, CDA for cooperatives
  2. Secure your Barangay Business Clearance and Mayor’s Permit
  3. Apply for BIR registration at your RDO or online (ORUS): file Form 1901 (sole proprietors, freelancers) or Form 1903 (corporations, partnerships, cooperatives). If you already have a TIN, use it
  4. Pay the ₱30 documentary stamp tax and receive your Certificate of Registration (COR, Form 2303). The ₱500 annual registration fee no longer applies
  5. Apply for an Authority to Print (Form 1906) for your invoices, register your books of accounts, and display your COR

Requirements vary by business type.

Need help with business registration? Contact us for hassle-free assistance!

What tax obligations do I have as a small business owner?
  • Monthly or quarterly income tax returns
  • Percentage tax or VAT returns
  • Annual Income Tax Return (ITR)
  • Books of accounts and financial statements
  • Withholding taxes
What happens if I pay my taxes late?

Late payment carries a 25% surcharge, 12% annual interest, and other penalties.

When do I need to transition to VAT?

If your gross sales exceed PHP 3 million, you must register as a VAT taxpayer. Contact us if you need help with the transition.

What are the bookkeeping requirements for businesses?

Businesses must keep books of accounts, manual or computerized, registered with the BIR. Contact us if you need help setting them up.
